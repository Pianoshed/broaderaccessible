Who We Are

Broader Accessible-Health Initiatives (BRAIN) is a registered, not-for-profit, and non-political organization dedicated to advancing equitable healthcare access and strengthening health systems across Nigeria. Founded on the principles of accountability, collaboration, and impact-driven service, BRAIN works to bridge critical gaps in healthcare delivery—particularly for vulnerable and underserved populations.

Our mission is to deliver sustainable, evidence-based solutions that improve health governance, service delivery, and community health outcomes. We focus on maternal, newborn, and child health (MNCH), reproductive health, malaria containment, and HIV/AIDS care.

Through strategic partnerships with government ministries, local health authorities, and international development partners, we support primary health facilities with technical assistance, field advocacy, and digital data infrastructure to ensure continuous, people-centered care.

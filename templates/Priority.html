Priority Health Programs

Our Core Scope

Comprehensive Clinical Portfolios

From preventive advocacy to clinical facility support, here is where we focus our resources.

🤝

Community Health Systems

Empowering local health committees, village health workers, and traditional leaders to build grassroots ownership for long-term health outcomes.

🏥

Primary Health Care Revitalization

Upgrading primary healthcare centers through workforce capacity building, essential medical equipment, and integrated service delivery workflows.

🔬

Infectious Disease Surveillance

Early detection, laboratory diagnostic networking, and emergency response frameworks to contain outbreak threats at the community level.

🦟

Malaria Elimination

Comprehensive seasonal chemoprevention, distribution of insecticide-treated bed nets, and rapid diagnostic testing (RDT) in rural endemic zones.

🎗️

HIV/AIDS Prevention & Care

Community-based HIV counseling and testing, antiretroviral therapy (ART) adherence support, stigma mitigation, and prevention of mother-to-child transmission.

🩺

Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)

Addressing chronic conditions including hypertension and diabetes through routine screenings, health literacy, and primary clinic medication continuity.

💊

Reproductive Health & Family Planning

Empowering women and couples with informed choice, voluntary modern contraception access, and reproductive health rights advocacy.

👶

Maternal and Child Health (MNCH)

Ensuring safe deliveries, training skilled birth attendants in emergency obstetric care, and newborn resuscitation to reduce preventable mortality.

🥗

Maternal & Child Nutrition

Community-level nutritional status surveillance, infant and young child feeding (IYCF) counselling, and targeted micronutrient supplementation.

Field Documentation

Our Programs in Action

Field missions, clinical outreach sessions, and training workshops across supported facilities.
